About Me

I'm Thomas You, a photographer based between North Carolina and Guangzhou. My work focuses on urban and natural landscapes, exploring spaces where environments and people intersect. With a background in both digital and film photography, I approach each project as an act of visual storytelling—quiet, direct, and image-first.
